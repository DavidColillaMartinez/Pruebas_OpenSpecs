# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners planning bathroom, kitchen, or full-home renovations in Spain. They are researching products before making purchase decisions — seeking clarity, trust, and visual confidence in a category (bathroom fittings) that is usually an afterthought in construction. Their context is practical (measurements, installation, durability) but their decision is emotional (will this look good? will it feel right?).

## Product Purpose

Bath Studio sells bathroom products (shower screens, shower trays, taps, accessories) with technical advice and a curated visual approach. The landing page exists to convert research into consultations: a visitor arrives unsure, leaves with a clear visual path through their renovation project. Success is measured by consultation requests and time-on-page (engagement with the product story).

## Brand Personality

Sobrio, preciso, premium.

Restrained, precise, premium. The voice is confident and understated — never hypey, never salesy. Think Aesop / Le Labo: warm-minimal, textured surfaces, type-driven. The brand communicates expertise through clarity and restraint, not through superlatives. Spanish-native tone: direct, warm but not effusive, professional but not cold.

## Anti-references

No generic SaaS landing pages. No startup-marketing templates (hero-metric grids, tiny uppercase eyebrow labels above every section, identical card grids, gradient text, glassmorphism as a default). Avoid rustic warmth (farmhouse wood tones) and sterile coldness (all-white hospital minimalism).

## Design Principles

1. **Presence through restraint.** Every element earns its place. Fewer things, better made. The page breathes — generous whitespace, deliberate pacing, no decorative filler.

2. **Tactile surfaces, not flat screens.** Materials read as real: layered shadows, soft gradients, rounded corners that feel physical. Borrow from bathroom material language (stone, glaze, brushed metal, misted glass) translated into CSS.

3. **Type as architecture.** Large, precise typography carries the visual weight. Display type at bold sizes with tight tracking; body copy airy and generous. Hierarchy is sculptural, not informational.

4. **One clear action per view.** Each section has exactly one job. The hero sells the visit; categories sell exploration; the contact section sells the consultation. No competing CTAs.

5. **Motion that reveals, not decorates.** Animations explain the product (panels assembling, surfaces completing, water flowing) rather than adding flash. Every movement serves comprehension.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practices: semantic HTML, sufficient color contrast (≥4.5:1 body, ≥3:1 large text), keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`. No specific WCAG level required beyond good-faith AA-level effort.
