## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The page fails the AI slop test decisively. Hero-metric grid, identical card grid, eyebrow labels on every section, glassmorphism sprawl (8 unauthorized backdrop-blur instances), Clay Rarity Rule broken, zero product imagery. The information architecture itself is a SaaS landing page skeleton dressed in mineral colors.

**Deterministic scan**: 1 warning — Inter flagged as overused font in `src/styles.css:8`. No other antipatterns detected by automated scanner (contrast, typography, spacing all passed).

## Overall Impression

The page has the bones of something distinctive but wears a SaaS boilerplate skeleton. The single biggest opportunity: strip the SaaS scaffolding and let the material metaphor breathe.

## What's Working

1. **ProductPanel CSS composition**: Genuinely original layered gradients with reveal-slab clip-path animation.
2. **Method section dark inversion**: Full-ink background with the strongest headline on the page.
3. **Typography as architecture**: Inter at 600 weight with tight tracking delivers structural precision.

## Priority Issues

### P0 — Hero metric grid must go (App.jsx:141-145)
Directly contradicts PRODUCT.md anti-reference. Delete the metric div entirely.

### P0 — Auto-rotating carousel has zero user control (App.jsx:220-224)
No pause, no manual controls. Add hover-to-pause, clickable dots, or make manual-only.

### P1 — 8 unauthorized backdrop-blur instances (App.jsx:80,89,124,142,143,144,200)
DESIGN.md limits glassmorphism to nav bar only. Strip backdrop-blur from all non-nav elements.

### P1 — Uppercase eyebrow labels on every section (App.jsx:163,190,232,260)
Four sections, four kickers. Keep only Metodo; remove the rest.

### P2 — Invalid HTML development artifact (App.jsx:124)
Button wrapping p with invalid installurl attribute. Remove button wrapper.

### P3 — Category cards have no product imagery (App.jsx:171-172)
Gradient placeholders in every card. A bathroom products page needs product photos.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: Dual CTA paths compete; ProductPanel abstraction is indecipherable; no product photos to anchor the category.

**Casey (Mobile)**: No mobile navigation (links hidden behind md:flex, no hamburger menu). Three-column metric grid breaks on 320px viewport.

**Riley (Stress Tester)**: Carousel race condition on rapid clicking; reduced-motion CSS/JS desync creates visual glitches.

## Minor Observations

No footer, no favicon, no OG/Twitter meta tags, missing scroll-padding-top for nav offset, motion tempo mismatch (700ms/900ms/2600ms), hero heading collision risk on narrow viewports.

## Questions to Consider

1. The information architecture IS a SaaS landing page skeleton. Is the structure itself wrong for a bathroom brand?
2. If a competitor had three product photos and you had none, who wins the renovation contract?
3. If Inter was replaced with a font that feels like poured concrete or cut stone, would the page gain conviction?
4. What if the Showcase was a single editorial spread with zero motion?
